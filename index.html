ABOUT US

NEIC – NERIST Entrepreneurship and Innovation Club is a student-driven platform that nurtures creative thinkers and aspiring entrepreneurs. We inspire innovation, empower leadership, and transform ideas into impactful ventures that shape the future.

VISION

NEIC nurtures a culture of creativity and entrepreneurship at NERIST, empowering students to turn bold ideas into impactful ventures through mentorship, collaboration, and hands-on opportunities.

PRESIDENT'S MESSAGE

"At NEIC, we believe innovation means creating something meaningful. Our mission is to build a community where ideas evolve into impact, and students develop the confidence to realize their vision. Together, we nurture not just startups, but the leadership and problem-solving skills that shape future innovators."

ROCKTABH PHUKAN
President, NEIC
